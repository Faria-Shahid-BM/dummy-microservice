PART A — COLLATERAL TYPE CLASSIFICATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Identify collateral from: property_description, goods_description,
policy_type, policy_class, BANK_POLICY_TEXT.
A single policy may cover multiple categories.

  [A] Building
      → Factory building, warehouse, commercial building,
        residential property

  [B] Plant & Machinery
      → Manufacturing machinery, production equipment,
        industrial plant, boilers, compressors

  [C] Inventory / Stock
      → Raw materials, finished goods, trading stock,
        inventory in storage, inventory in transit

  [D] Life Insurance – Borrower
      → Life policy assigned to bank, credit life,
        WBCA, key person life

  [E] Movable Assets
      → Vehicles, equipment, mobile assets, furniture
        & fixtures, portable machinery

If collateral type cannot be determined → classify as "Unknown",
status "Info Not Available", add Data Gap, apply all requirements
as "Info Not Available".

PART B — POLICY TYPE CLASSIFICATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Classify into exactly one code from INSURANCE_JSON:

  [P1]  Fire & Allied Perils
  [P2]  Property All Risks
  [P3]  Inland Transit
  [P4]  Marine Cargo Open Policy
  [P5]  Machinery Breakdown
  [P6]  Comprehensive Motor
  [P7]  Floater Policy
  [P8]  Life Insurance
  [P9]  Credit Life / WBCA
  [P10] Unknown

If undetermined → assign [P10], status "Info Not Available",
add Data Gap, apply all requirements as "Info Not Available".

PART C — COLLATERAL ↔ POLICY MATCH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Expected mapping:

  Building                → P1, P2
  Plant & Machinery       → P1, P2, P5
  Inventory (Storage)     → P1, P2
  Inventory (Transit)     → P3, P4
  Life Insurance Borrower → P8, P9
  Movable Assets          → P6, P7, P3 (if in movement)

If policy type NOT in expected mapping for detected collateral:
  → Non-Compliant | Critical | category: "Wrong Policy Type"
  → Raise dedicated issue_id

If either type is "Unknown" → policy_match_status:
"Info Not Available", no issue, add Data Gap.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PART D — COLLATERAL-SPECIFIC RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[A] BUILDING
   - Apply full peril checklist from BANK_POLICY_TEXT
     (Fire, Lightning, Explosion, Earthquake, RSD, SRCC,
      Atmospheric Disturbance, Aircraft Damage, etc.)
   - Check insured value vs. reinstatement / replacement value
   - Check bank named as mortgagee / beneficiary

[B] PLANT & MACHINERY
   - Apply full peril checklist from BANK_POLICY_TEXT
   - If Machinery Breakdown policy also present, check
     sudden & accidental breakdown coverage
   - Check bank named as loss payee

[C] INVENTORY / STOCK
   Storage:
   - Apply full peril checklist from BANK_POLICY_TEXT
   - Check sum insured vs. stock value / operative limit
   - Check bank named as beneficiary / loss payee

   Transit (if covered):
   - Apply transit-specific rules per Part E — P3/P4 below

[D] LIFE INSURANCE – BORROWER
   - Verify borrower is the life assured
   - Verify policy is assigned / endorsed to bank
   - Verify policy is active and not lapsed
   - Verify sum assured vs. outstanding facility amount
   - Check for surrender value restrictions

[E] MOVABLE ASSETS
   - Verify comprehensive cover (not third-party only)
   - Check bank named as loss payee
   - If assets moved between locations, check transit
     coverage applies

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PART E — POLICY-SPECIFIC RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[P1] FIRE & ALLIED PERILS / [P2] PROPERTY ALL RISKS
   - Check each required peril individually against risk_clauses
     per BANK_POLICY_TEXT peril list
   - Do NOT apply transit peril rules to storage policies

[P3] INLAND TRANSIT
   1. "Road/Rail Cargo Clauses A" = all-risks for inland transit.
      If present → mark comprehensive peril coverage Compliant.
      Do NOT create individual rows for Fire, Earthquake, RSD,
      Burglary, Atmospheric Disturbance, Aircraft Damage,
      Electrical, or Explosion — storage requirements only.

   2. SRCC: check for "Road/Rail Strikes Clauses Cargo" or
      equivalent. Absent → Non-Compliant, raise issue.

   3. Transit Terrorism: check for "Cargo Termination of Transit
      Clause (Terrorism)" or equivalent. Absent → Non-Compliant,
      raise issue.

   4. SEA VESSEL RULE: Inland Transit = within-country road/rail/
      air movement. Sea vessel coverage NOT applicable.
      Mark as "N/A". Never raise as issue or data gap.
      Only flag missing sea vessel coverage for international /
      ocean marine policies.

[P4] MARINE CARGO OPEN POLICY
   - Check Institute Cargo Clauses (A / B / C) or equivalent
   - Check sea vessel coverage (IS applicable and must be checked)
   - Check SRCC and War / Terrorism clauses

[P5] MACHINERY BREAKDOWN
   - Check sudden & accidental mechanical / electrical breakdown
   - Check business interruption extension if required by
     BANK_POLICY_TEXT

[P6] COMPREHENSIVE MOTOR
   - Verify comprehensive (not third-party only)
   - Check bank named as loss payee
   - Check no restrictive clauses excluding bank's interest

[P7] FLOATER POLICY
   - Check all asset locations are included
   - Check per-location and per-item limits are adequate
   - Check bank named as beneficiary / loss payee

[P8] LIFE INSURANCE / [P9] CREDIT LIFE / WBCA
   - Verify assignment to bank is endorsed on policy
   - Verify no lapse or premium default
   - Verify borrower is the life assured

[P10] UNKNOWN POLICY TYPE
   - Mark all peril and coverage checks "Info Not Available"
   - Add Data Gap entry
